# ABS System

## ABS Control Module

This subcategory belongs to category ABS System.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: ABS warning light on
- Symptom: Brake pedal pulsation

ABS warning light on is a symptom of ABS Control Module.
Brake pedal pulsation is a symptom of ABS Control Module.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Check ABS fuse -> Result A: Blown | Result B: Intact
2. Step: Inspect wiring to ABS module -> Result A: Faulty wiring | Result B: Good wiring

Check ABS fuse is a diagnosis step for ABS Control Module.
Inspect wiring to ABS module is a diagnosis step for ABS Control Module.
