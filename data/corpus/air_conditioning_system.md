# Air Conditioning System

## AC Compressor

This subcategory belongs to category Air Conditioning System.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: No cold air from vents
- Symptom: Loud noise when AC is on

No cold air from vents is a symptom of AC Compressor.
Loud noise when AC is on is a symptom of AC Compressor.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Check AC compressor clutch operation -> Result A: Clutch not engaging | Result B: Clutch engages properly
2. Step: Inspect AC compressor for leaks or damage -> Result A: Leaking/damaged compressor | Result B: Intact compressor

Check AC compressor clutch operation is a diagnosis step for AC Compressor.
Inspect AC compressor for leaks or damage is a diagnosis step for AC Compressor.
