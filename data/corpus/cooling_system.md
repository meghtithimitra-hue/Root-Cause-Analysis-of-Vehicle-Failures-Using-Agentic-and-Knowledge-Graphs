# Cooling System

## Coolant Leak Diagnosis

This subcategory belongs to category Cooling System.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Visible coolant puddle under vehicle
- Symptom: Coolant odor in cabin

Visible coolant puddle under vehicle is a symptom of Coolant Leak Diagnosis.
Coolant odor in cabin is a symptom of Coolant Leak Diagnosis.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Pressure test cooling system -> Result A: Leaking system | Result B: No leaks detected
2. Step: Inspect radiator, hoses, and water pump for leaks -> Result A: Leaking component found | Result B: Components are intact

Pressure test cooling system is a diagnosis step for Coolant Leak Diagnosis.
Inspect radiator, hoses, and water pump for leaks is a diagnosis step for Coolant Leak Diagnosis.
