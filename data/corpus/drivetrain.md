# Drivetrain

## Bevel Gears

This subcategory belongs to category Drivetrain.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Whining noise from drivetrain
- Symptom: Difficulty shifting gears

Whining noise from drivetrain is a symptom of Bevel Gears.
Difficulty shifting gears is a symptom of Bevel Gears.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Inspect bevel gears for wear or damage -> Result A: Worn/damaged gears | Result B: Gears in good condition
2. Step: Check gear oil level and condition -> Result A: Low/dirty gear oil | Result B: Sufficient/clean gear oil

Inspect bevel gears for wear or damage is a diagnosis step for Bevel Gears.
Check gear oil level and condition is a diagnosis step for Bevel Gears.
