# Emissions System

## Canister Purge Valve

This subcategory belongs to category Emissions System.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Check engine light on
- Symptom: Rough idle

Check engine light on is a symptom of Canister Purge Valve.
Rough idle is a symptom of Canister Purge Valve.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Test canister purge valve operation -> Result A: Valve stuck open/closed | Result B: Valve operates correctly
2. Step: Check for leaks in purge valve hoses -> Result A: Leaking hoses | Result B: No leaks

Test canister purge valve operation is a diagnosis step for Canister Purge Valve.
Check for leaks in purge valve hoses is a diagnosis step for Canister Purge Valve.
