## Air Filter

This subcategory belongs to category Engine Compartment.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Decreased engine performance
- Symptom: Black smoke from exhaust

Decreased engine performance is a symptom of Air Filter.
Black smoke from exhaust is a symptom of Air Filter.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Inspect air filter for clogs or dirt -> Result A: Clogged/dirty filter | Result B: Clean filter
2. Step: Replace air filter if necessary -> Result A: Replace filter | Result B: Filter in good condition

Inspect air filter for clogs or dirt is a diagnosis step for Air Filter.
Replace air filter if necessary is a diagnosis step for Air Filter.
