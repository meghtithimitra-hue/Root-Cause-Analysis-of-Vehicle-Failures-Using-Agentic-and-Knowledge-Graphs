# Fuel System

## Fuel Injector

This subcategory belongs to category Fuel System.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Engine misfires
- Symptom: Poor fuel economy

Engine misfires is a symptom of Fuel Injector.
Poor fuel economy is a symptom of Fuel Injector.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Test fuel injector spray pattern -> Result A: Irregular spray pattern | Result B: Uniform spray pattern
2. Step: Inspect injector for leaks or clogs -> Result A: Leaking/clogged injector | Result B: Clean/in-tolerance injector

Test fuel injector spray pattern is a diagnosis step for Fuel Injector.
Inspect injector for leaks or clogs is a diagnosis step for Fuel Injector.
