# Liquid Systems

## Brake Fluid

This subcategory belongs to category Liquid Systems.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Soft brake pedal
- Symptom: Brake warning light on

Soft brake pedal is a symptom of Brake Fluid.
Brake warning light on is a symptom of Brake Fluid.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Check brake fluid level and condition -> Result A: Low/dirty fluid | Result B: Clean/sufficient fluid
2. Step: Inspect brake lines for leaks -> Result A: Leaking lines | Result B: Lines in good condition

Check brake fluid level and condition is a diagnosis step for Brake Fluid.
Inspect brake lines for leaks is a diagnosis step for Brake Fluid.
