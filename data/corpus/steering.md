# Steering

## Steering Noise

This subcategory belongs to category Steering.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Noise when turning steering wheel

Noise when turning steering wheel is a symptom of Steering Noise.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Inspect power steering fluid level -> Result A: Low fluid level | Result B: Sufficient fluid level
2. Step: Check power steering pump and hoses for leaks -> Result A: Leaking pump/hoses | Result B: Pump and hoses in good condition

Inspect power steering fluid level is a diagnosis step for Steering Noise.
Check power steering pump and hoses for leaks is a diagnosis step for Steering Noise.
