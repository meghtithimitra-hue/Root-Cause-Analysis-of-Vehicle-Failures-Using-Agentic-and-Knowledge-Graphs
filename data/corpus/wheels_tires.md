# Wheels & Tires

## Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS)

This subcategory belongs to category Wheels & Tires.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: TPMS warning light on
- Symptom: Low tire pressure

TPMS warning light on is a symptom of Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS).
Low tire pressure is a symptom of Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS).

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Check tire pressures with gauge -> Result A: Low/high pressure | Result B: Correct pressure
2. Step: Inspect TPMS sensors for damage or corrosion -> Result A: Damaged/corroded sensors | Result B: Clean/intact sensors

Check tire pressures with gauge is a diagnosis step for Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS).
Inspect TPMS sensors for damage or corrosion is a diagnosis step for Tire Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS).

## Wheel Bearing

This subcategory belongs to category Wheels & Tires.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Grinding noise from wheels
- Symptom: Uneven tire wear

Grinding noise from wheels is a symptom of Wheel Bearing.
Uneven tire wear is a symptom of Wheel Bearing.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Check wheel bearing for play -> Result A: Loose/worn bearing | Result B: Bearing in good condition
2. Step: Inspect bearing for signs of damage or corrosion -> Result A: Damaged/corroded bearing | Result B: Clean/intact bearing

Check wheel bearing for play is a diagnosis step for Wheel Bearing.
Inspect bearing for signs of damage or corrosion is a diagnosis step for Wheel Bearing.

## Wheel Hub

This subcategory belongs to category Wheels & Tires.

### Symptoms

- Symptom: Clicking or popping noise when turning
- Symptom: Wheel wobbling

Clicking or popping noise when turning is a symptom of Wheel Hub.
Wheel wobbling is a symptom of Wheel Hub.

### Diagnosis Steps

1. Step: Inspect wheel hub for play or bearing noise -> Result A: Loose/worn hub | Result B: Hub in good condition
2. Step: Check wheel bearings for wear -> Result A: Worn bearings | Result B: Bearings in good condition

Inspect wheel hub for play or bearing noise is a diagnosis step for Wheel Hub.
Check wheel bearings for wear is a diagnosis step for Wheel Hub.
